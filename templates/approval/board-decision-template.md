## Next Steps

### Immediate Actions (within 5 business days)
1. Notify submitter and sponsor of decision
2. Distribute decision document to stakeholders
3. Update submission status in SharePoint
4. Archive decision and supporting documents
5. Update ARB metrics and dashboards

### For Submitter/Project Team
**If Approved:**
1. Begin implementation planning
2. Schedule regular ARB status updates
3. Report any deviations immediately
4. Schedule post-implementation review

**If Conditional Approval:**
1. Create plan to address conditions
2. Schedule condition verification
3. Submit evidence of compliance
4. Obtain ARB sign-off before proceeding

**If Rejected:**
1. Review rejection reasons
2. Decide on next steps (resubmit, alternative approach, appeal)
3. If resubmitting, address all concerns
4. If appealing, submit within 15 business days
