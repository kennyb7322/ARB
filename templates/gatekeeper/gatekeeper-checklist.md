## Appendix: Gatekeeper Guidelines

### Review Time Guidelines
- Simple submissions: 1-2 hours
- Moderate submissions: 2-4 hours
- Complex submissions: 4-8 hours

### When to RETURN
- Missing required information
- Poor quality diagrams
- Unclear business justification
- Incomplete cost analysis
- Missing required attachments

### When to REJECT
- Does not align with enterprise strategy
- Violates architecture principles
- Unacceptable security risks
- Insufficient business justification
- Not appropriate for ARB review

### When to FAST-TRACK
- Executive priority
- Emergency/critical need
- Low complexity and risk
- Uses standard patterns
- Clear business value

### Escalation Triggers
- Conflict of interest
- Uncertainty about decision
- Political sensitivity
- High cost or risk
- Strategic importance
